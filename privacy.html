Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 3, 2026

1. Introduction

Monster Media Buyer ("we", "our", "us") operates the website monstermediabuyer.pp.ua and provides a SaaS platform that connects Meta Ads accounts to Claude AI via MCP (Model Context Protocol). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

2. Information We Collect

When you use our service, we may collect: your name and email address provided during registration; Facebook/Meta access tokens required to connect your ad accounts; usage data such as which features you use and how often.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to: provide and maintain our service; connect your Meta Ads accounts to Claude AI; process your subscription payments; communicate with you about service updates.

4. Meta Ads Data

We access your Meta Ads data solely to provide analytics, reporting, and campaign management features through Claude AI. We do not sell, share, or use your advertising data for any purpose other than delivering our service to you. Your Meta access token is stored securely on encrypted servers and is used only for API requests you initiate.

5. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on secure servers hosted by Railway. We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest. Access tokens are stored securely and never exposed to third parties.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share data only with: service providers necessary to operate our platform (hosting, payment processing); law enforcement when required by law.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us. Upon account deletion, we remove your Meta access tokens and personal data within 30 days. When you disconnect a Meta ad account or revoke access, we immediately delete the associated token.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to: access your personal data; correct inaccurate data; request deletion of your data; withdraw consent at any time; export your data.

9. Cookies

We use essential cookies only to maintain your login session. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page.
